📜 Terms & Conditions (Niyam aur Shartein)

Please read these Terms & Conditions carefully before participating in the FF Diamond Watch & Earn platform.

  • 1. Public Browsing & Player Accounts Any visitor can freely browse the Home page, watch the Platform Introduction Video, read Terms & Conditions, or send a Promotional Inquiry without logging in. To earn Diamonds, claim daily missions, or request a Free Fire UID top-up, you must register a free player account with a valid numeric Free Fire UID (5–16 digits).
  • 2. Verified Watch-to-Earn Rules (1x Speed Only) Standard videos and Shorts credit 0.15 💎 per 60 seconds of verified real playback, while videos marked with 🔥 2X Boost credit 0.30 💎 per 60 seconds. Playback speed is strictly locked to 1x; pausing, seeking/fast-forwarding, switching tabs, or muting/backgrounding the browser window earns 0 Diamonds.
  • 3. Lifetime Per-Video Reward Cap (No Loop Farming) Each player can earn the full duration reward of a specific video once in their lifetime (100% progress bar). Replaying or looping a completed video is welcome for entertainment, but does not generate additional Diamonds.
  • 4. Mandatory 10-Minute Activity Check After every 10 minutes of continuous playback, an "Are you still watching?" confirmation pops up for 20 seconds. Reward tracking pauses until you confirm.
  • 5. Fraud-Proof Referral Policy When you invite a friend using your Referral Code, the +2.00 💎 referral bonus is credited to both accounts only after the invited friend completes 15 minutes of verified video watch time. Creating fake or multiple self-referral accounts will result in permanent account suspension.
  • 6. Diamond Redemptions & Owner Verification The minimum redemption is 100 💎. When you request a top-up pack, your balance is placed on hold while the platform owner reviews your watch history and activity logs. Approved requests are sent directly to your registered Free Fire UID; rejected requests automatically refund legitimate balances or forfeit fraudulent balances.
